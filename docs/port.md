# The port (`src/game`, `src/render`, `play.html`)

The first playable slice: drive one car on one track, seen from the driver's seat. Open `play.html` (dev: `npm run dev`, then *Load local game*; hosted: *Open WACKY.DAT*). Everything is decoded in the browser from the player's own archive.

## Clock, start and turning (proven, 2026-10-04)

- The horizon strip scrolls with the steering ramps (`horizon.scroll`, `src/game/horizon.js`, replayed on 410 recorded pairs by `port-horizon`). A steering tick turns the camera about the point NDIST[0] units ahead of it and the move uses the old heading (`car.turn-move`, replayed by `port-carmodel`).

- The logic runs one frame per 12 counts of a 136 Hz counter, 88.2 ms (`tick.rate`, `src/game/clock.js`); the HUD race timer is a separate 10 Hz counter. The start heading is 480 and the start camera is the player's position less 0x7c in y (`race.start-heading`).

## What is not ported or not known (shown on the page)

- The main menu is complete as far as it was observed. Not observed: the state its animations start in when it is entered (`UNKNOWN_MENU_START_STATE`), a held key's repeat and Esc during the icons' animation (`UNKNOWN_MENU_HELD_KEYS`). SINGLE PLAYER RACING leads through the class, laps, engine and race type menus (`obs-menuflow`, record `menuflow-1`) into the race, with the engine chosen there (the vehicle screen, the cup board and the track preview are not ported: the page's track is used); GAME OPTIONS, COMM-BAT PLAY, WACKY WHEELS INFO, GRAPHIC DETAIL LEVELS and RACE SCREEN OPTIONS (three ON/OFF switches) open their menus, and the detail lists set the detail level; every other item (the screens behind VOLUME LEVELS, MODEM and SERIAL CABLE, the races, ORDERING, INSTRUCTIONS, FAMOUS DRIVERS and CREDITS, ORDERING INFO and LEAVE GAME) is recorded as pending in `src/game/menus.js` and says so on the page. The earlier belief that the menu is drawn over WINTRO.PCX (from `boot-1`'s sample at 32 s) was refuted by the live screen: it is CHECK.PCX, as the lead said.

- The HUD is ported in part: the speedometer, the place digit, the timer, the top banner, the driver name, the lap text and the reaction figure. Shown as unproven on the page: the place digit (always 1st: no other car exists in the port), the count label (0) and the single digit (3), whose start values were read in fresh races but whose meaning is unknown, the name TIGI (the vehicle screen is not ported), the laps from the menu choice (6, 8 or 10, read from the code), and the last three calls of the reaction figure (their pictures, the table at obj2+0xa538, are not identified: the port draws nothing). Not ported: the minimap (its picture is a screen copy taken after the medal picture is drawn, which needs the cup board; the dots are proven and not drawn on nothing), the held-item icon (no pickups), the finish and what follows it (the car's stop, the places, the end screens: the timer freezes at the final time and the text reads FINISHED!, and the race goes on), the countdown (the race mode word is always 2) and TIME TRIAL (six laps and the timer rule).
- The AI cars (their pictures, slots and frames), pickups and consumption of objects, sound, laps, the AI and items: not ported yet (see `docs/next-steps.md`).
- Accelerate and brake together, and the slowdown off the road (observed in `car.speed-observed`, not ported yet).
