# The port (`src/game`, `src/render`, `play.html`)

The first playable slice: drive one car on one track, seen from the driver's seat. Open `play.html` (dev: `npm run dev`, then *Load local game*; hosted: *Open WACKY.DAT*). Everything is decoded in the browser from the player's own archive. `play.html?debug` exposes `window.wackyDebug` (the race state and the frame functions) so a test can step the race by hand; browsers pause the animation frames of a hidden tab.

## Clock, start and turning (proven, 2026-10-04)

- The horizon strip scrolls with the steering ramps (`horizon.scroll`, `src/game/horizon.js`, replayed on 410 recorded pairs by `port-horizon`). A steering tick turns the camera about the point NDIST[0] units ahead of it and the move uses the old heading (`car.turn-move`, replayed by `port-carmodel`).

- The logic runs one frame per 12 counts of a 136 Hz counter, 88.2 ms (`tick.rate`, `src/game/clock.js`); the HUD race timer is a separate 10 Hz counter. The start heading is 480 and the start camera is the player's position less 0x7c in y (`race.start-heading`).

## The rest of the game around the race (STATIC, read from the disassembly, 2026-10-04)

All of it enters through claims (`docs/TODO.md` lists each with its gap). Replays: `port-lean` (the control routine's state machine replays the lean of `carsteer-1`), `port-shots` (the fire key, the shots and the hit words of `shot-1` to `shot-3`), `port-carmodel`, `port-drive`, `port-contact`, `port-finish` and `port-start` still pass through the new car step.

- `src/game/control.js` (`car.control-rules`): the player's control and input routines, the animation states and picture tables; `car.js` walks the move with the exact Bresenham of `walk.js`, bounces on walls (clang and spark), jumps over pits, boosts, starts the special surface flag.
- `src/game/shots.js` (`item.shot-rules`): the fire key, the 61 shot records, the hits on computer racers (stun 33 and 50 calls) and the human; `ai.js` skips hit racers and applies the class level and catch-up threshold of `classes.js` (`race.class-levels`, the kid mode too).
- `src/game/race.js`: finishing places, the labels with the driver pictures, the message and the points (`race.finish-flow`), then the leaderboard (`race.leaderboard`) 800 counts later or the menu of a lost race.
- The screens: vehicle screen (`menu.vehicle-screen`), cup board (`race.cup-board`), loading card and track preview, the cup of five races (`race.cup-flow`), leaderboard; scenes live in `src/play.js`.
- `src/game/effects.js` (`effects.ground-dust`, `effects.car-rear`, `effects.sparks`, `effects.pit`): what the draw of the player's entry adds around the car, once a frame after each call: the car's one-row jiggle (the observed top rows 164 and 165), the wheel dust of EFFECTS.SP chosen through the terrain's WACKY.SDX record and the frame of the car (the entries 1 to 3 and 5 to 7 of the slot record give the first picture at the table's x offsets, every other frame the two 10 x 9 puffs or the 12 x 24 splash), the three pictures at the car's rear (HOGMIS.SP), the sparks (SPARK.SP) and the pit surfaces 6 and 15 (the 38 x 28 splash, the 32 x 24 car in the pit state, its end). The blits ride on the player's draw-list record (`after`), so nearer objects still cover them. Where the record has no picture loaded for the track (the flag byte is 0: terrain 0 on tracks 2, 4, 11, 12, 13, 24, 28, 32 to 35) the game draws from an unloaded pointer; the port draws nothing and says so on the page. The kind 1 pickup's tyre strips and the tyre on the car (`effects.tyres`, GENEF.SP) run as the game's state machine, and `port-tyres` replays the 60 samples of `tyres-1` (state, the three strip words and the strips' start row 158 from the projected entry). The head-on obstacle crash follows the car routine's states (`effects.crash`): the car flies up the screen in eight driver frames, holds, falls with the fall frame, is gone, then costs a try and the same race starts again (or the game is over); from the hold on the HUD tail draws CRACK.SP and seven Bresenham lines (`src/render/crack.js`). Not ported: the crash by a head-on racer and the two-player resets.
- The chosen vehicle: the race car uses the chosen driver's slot of CARS.SP and the driver's own file (`driverFile`, the file names of `racer.driver-slots`), and the waiting car the slot's idle frames.
- `src/game/blink.js` (`racer.blink`): the object pass of the draw: a computer racer the player trails closely and sees exactly from behind glances back (its frames 8, 9, 9, 9, 9, 8, or 10, 11, ... for one left of the player's column) with its driver voice, and the player's car glances at a racer level with or behind it (the bump pictures and the sound PASS). The observed idle frames of the start (`race.idle-observed`) stay for the first 41 ticks; the pass takes over after them.
- `src/game/rostrum.js` + `src/render/rostrum.js` (`race.rostrum`): after the final leaderboard, THE ROSTRUM: the title on the plain ORDER.PCX background for 400 counts, then WIN.PCX with the puffs, the portraits (the human in its finish pose), the wheel and the three falling cups with their landing sounds, one pass every 6 counts; LEADRBRD plays once and MAINMENU follows. 
- `src/game/halloffame.js` + `src/render/famous.js` (`race.hall-of-fame`): the winner of the final standings types a name of up to 12 letters, digits or spaces (the gold cup marks the next place), the total enters the class's four records by the game's rule (the tables are WAC1.HTT or WAC2.HTT of the archive, kept in this browser's storage after a change as the game keeps them in WACKY1.HI or WACKY2.HI), and the FAMOUS DRIVERS board shows them (LEAD.PCX, the class box, the driver's car, the date, the cup's wheel and the points). Not ported yet: the Info menu's path to the board, the time trial hall of fame, the bonus notice.
- `src/game/timetrial.js` + `src/render/trial.js` (`race.time-trial`): the TIME TRIAL class: the cup board's track choice (fire lights the race's label, the arrows change it, fire shows its hall of fame, any key but Esc starts the race), the race alone for six laps with the lap times of the 10 Hz timer, and the hall of fame after it: the best lap and the whole race checked against the four lap records and the race record of the track (the 30 records of WAC1.OTT or WAC2.OTT, kept in this browser's storage after a change), the name entry (16 characters) for a record, the screen with the laps, the records and the blinking markers, and the boxes for the best lap and race ever.
- The setup around the race: the class item's forced items (`setup.forced-items`: the time trial skips the laps menu and runs 6 laps, the kid mode skips the laps and engine menus and runs 3 laps with the 6 HP engines) and the kid mode's nine-line explanation box (`menu.kid-notice`); the Info menu's FAMOUS DRIVERS menus for the race boards and the time trial halls (`menu.famous-menus`); SAVE AND QUIT and RESTORE SAVED GAME (`race.save-game`: the record of each cup race's start kept in this browser's storage, the restore menu in front of the vehicle screen); and the pickup counter and held item that go from one race of a cup to the next (obj1+0x2006a).
- Sound (`audio.race-sounds`, `src/play-audio.js`): the numbered VOC sounds with the game's priorities and eight voices, the looping engine whose pitch follows the speed, the songs (KLM rendered in a worker). The race frames emit `state.sounds`.
