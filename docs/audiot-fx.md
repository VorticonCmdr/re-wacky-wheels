# FX — AUDIOT sound archive

audiot.fx stores uncompressed, unnamed chunks; [AUDIOHED](audiohed-fx.md) supplies boundaries. Account for `!ID!` section tags; not every slot is a playable sound.

Wacky Wheels documents 26 [PC-speaker](pc-speaker.md) and 26 [AdLib](adlib-sfx.md) effects, with corresponding indices. Both FX files are members of WACKY.DAT.
