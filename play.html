W Wacky Wheels PLAYTEST

Choose your own WACKY.DAT (nothing is uploaded) to drive a track.

Graphics

Original is the reference renderer. Enhanced keeps the original artwork and resolution and adds smooth motion between the game's 11 ticks a second, shadows, particles, skid marks and fog: presentation only, the race runs the same either way. The same settings are in the game's menu: GAME OPTIONS, ENHANCED GRAPHICS.

Arrows steer, Alt (or Up, A) accelerates, Down (or Z) brakes, Space is the hand brake turn, Ctrl fires a hog or drops the held item (not ported yet), R restarts the race.
